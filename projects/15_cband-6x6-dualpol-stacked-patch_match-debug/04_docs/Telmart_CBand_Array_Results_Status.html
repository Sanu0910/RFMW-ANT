1Summary of results

> 20 dBIsolation between V and H inputs over most of the band (array)
Main result. The poor input match reported on the array is caused by the radiating element itself, whose matched bandwidth is about 16 %, well below the 54 % needed for 4–7 GHz. The radiator works correctly inside its band, which is why the gain is as expected while the VSWR is poor.
Consequence. A change to the feed network alone cannot bring the VSWR below 3:1 across 4–7 GHz. A new wideband radiating element is required; its development has started (section 5).

4Complete array results (preliminary)

  • Both inputs are best in the middle of the band (about 5.0–6.2 GHz).
  • Below about 4.8 GHz and above about 6.3 GHz both inputs are mostly at VSWR 3:1 or worse, consistent with the element result.
  • Isolation between the V and H inputs is better than 20 dB over most of the band.
  • The array result confirms that the element bandwidth is the limiting factor.

5Element development (in progress)

A new wideband element is being developed to meet VSWR < 3 (target < 2) over the full 4–7 GHz band on both polarisations. Iteration 1 has been completed; it is matched from 4.95 to 5.75 GHz and has defined the design direction for iteration 2.

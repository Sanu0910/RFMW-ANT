## What it is
An open, lightweight machined base plate for each X-band THRU cal board in `00_input/MSL_SL_Cal_Lines.zip` (RO4350B 30 mil, MSL launch -> via -> stripline -> via -> MSL). Two connector walls carry the same HUBER+SUHNER 23_SMA-50-0-272/133_N (item 23030310) used on the project 01 divider housing; the board drops into a locating pocket and is held by its own 4 x M3 holes.

## Notes
- The pocket ends are the connector walls: the 5.09 mm spigot stands 0.09 proud of the 5.0 wall, leaving 0.11 mm to the board end, and the 2.92 mm pin then covers 2.51 mm of launch copper.
- The plate is open (no lid). The stripline section is shielded by the board's via fence; the MSL launches radiate a little, which is part of what the THRU calibrates.
- Underside lightening keeps a 3.0 mm rib on the line axis, 2.5 mm side rims and R4.0 bosses round the M3 taps; the wall zones stay solid for the M2.5 flange taps.
